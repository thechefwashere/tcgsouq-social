# How to verify your business in Meta Business Suite

This article explains how business portfolio owners with full control can verify their business in Meta Business Suite. Business verification is required to access certain advertising, developer, billing and spending features. It covers prerequisites, the step-by-step verification process, and what to do if your verification is rejected.

Learn more about [when you may need to verify your business](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1095661473946872).

## Prerequisites for verifying your business in Meta Business Suite

- You can only start the business verification process in Meta Business Suite if your business portfolio is [eligible](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1095661473946872) for verification. A business portfolio is where you manage your business assets on Meta.
- You must have [full control](https://facebook.com/business/help/442345745885606) of the [business portfolio](https://facebook.com/business/help/486932075688253). Full control is the highest permission level for a business portfolio, allowing you to manage all settings including verification."
- Depending on the product or feature you want to access that requires you to verify your business, you may be emailed or notified from a platform other than [Security Center](https://business.facebook.com/settings/security) to complete the verification process. In this case, refer to that email or notification for instructions.
- If you use [monthly invoicing](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2086865811541431) to pay for ads, then you don't need to submit documentation for business verification. When you start the business verification process, you can fill out the form with your credit line information and submit it. If you don't see that option, make sure that the business portfolio you're trying to verify is linked to a credit line that you own, not one that is shared with another business.
- If you provide false or misleading information during the verification process, attempt to claim and verify a business you’re not authorized to represent, or otherwise attempt to circumvent Meta’s verification review system, Meta may prevent your business from being verified, remove your business's verified status or take additional action on the account.
- Verifying your business in Meta Business Suite is different from [Meta Verified for businesses](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/308979828303560) and won’t give you a verified badge.

## How to verify your business in Meta Business Suite

To verify your business in Meta Business Suite using a desktop browser:

1. Go to [Security Center](https://business.facebook.com/settings/security) in Meta Business Suite.
   - If you cannot access this, then you may not have [full control](https://facebook.com/business/help/442345745885606) of the business portfolio.
2. Click **Start verification**.
   - If you see **Ineligible for verification**, then you don’t need to complete verification in Security Center. However, depending on the product or feature you want to access that requires you to verify your business, you may be emailed or notified from a platform other than [Security Center](https://business.facebook.com/settings/security) to complete the verification process. In this case, refer to that email or notification for instructions.
3. Provide your business details, including the legal business name, address, phone number and website. Check that the details you enter exactly match the details of your legal business entity. Also make sure your business website loads and is HTTPS compliant (this means your website uses a secure connection and the URL starts with https://).
4. Confirm your business details. If no matching record is found, then you can select **My business isn’t listed** or **None of these match**. In this case, you may be asked to upload [official documents](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/159334372093366), such as a business license or articles of incorporation, to confirm the details you entered.
5. Choose a method (email, phone, text message, WhatsApp message or domain verification) to confirm your connection. To confirm via domain verification (which confirms you own your business's website by adding a meta tag or DNS record), [follow the domain verification steps in the Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/321167023127050).
   - Learn how to [edit your business details](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/322526208728282) such as business phone number.
6. Confirm your connection, then click **Done**. You'll see a confirmation that your verification submission has been received.

A decision on your verification submission may take up to 14 business days. You’ll receive a notification when the review is complete. If you receive confirmation that your business is verified, then no further action is needed. Learn [what to do if your application was rejected](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2342133782492969).

**Note:** If you [edit your business details](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/322526208728282), then you need to complete the verification process again.
