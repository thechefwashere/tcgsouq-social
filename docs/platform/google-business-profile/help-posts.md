# Create & manage posts on your Business Profile

You can post updates on your Business Profile to share announcements, offers, updates, and event details directly with your customers on Search and Maps. When customers find your latest business updates on your Business Profile, it helps them decide to visit your business.

Post on your Business Profile to:

- Share up-to-date info and improve your customer experience.
- Promote your sales, specials, events, news, and offers.
- Connect with your customers through posts with photos and videos.

Understand where customers can find your posts

Posts can show text, photos, or videos, and customers can find them in:

- The “Updates” or “Overview” tabs of the Business Profile on mobile on Search and Maps.
- The “From the owner” section of the Business Profile on your computer on Search and Maps.
- Featured posts that are added directly to your Business Profile on mobile.

Posts about events, deals, or specials may automatically show on your Business Profile on mobile. These featured posts are from your social media channels, like Facebook and Instagram, or from posts you've created on your Business Profile.

**Tips:**

- This feature is currently available in English for single-location food and drink businesses in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand.
- When featured posts are available, customers find them in place of reviews on your Business Profile on mobile. They can still access reviews when they tap your business's star rating or check the "Reviews" tab.

Understand the different types of posts

**Important:** Posts that include a phone number in the post description might get rejected. To add additional phone numbers to your profile, [learn more about edits to your Business Profile](/business/answer/3039617#phone).

You can create and customize these types of posts:

- **Updates:** Share info about your business.
  - You can add a description, photo, video, and an action button with a link. The button should direct customers to a website where they can complete the action, like make a booking, order online, or learn more.
- **Offers:** Provide promotional sales or deals from your business.
  - It requires a title, dates, and time. This automatically includes a "View Offer" button on the post. You can also include a description, photo, video, coupon code, link, and terms and conditions.
- **Events:** Promote an event at your business.
  - Add a title, start and end dates, and times. If you don’t provide a start or end time, the event shows as lasting 24 hours on the event dates. You can also include a description, photo, video, and an action button.

**Tip:** Posts older than 6 months are archived unless a date range is set.

## Create a post

1. [Go to your Business Profile](https://business.google.com/).
2. Select **Posts**  **Add post**.
3. Select the post type:
   - **Update**
   - **Offer**
   - **Event**
4. Select the elements you want to add to your post.
   - To add more details, select the available options, like Button or Coupon code.
5. Add info for your post.
   - If you want to schedule your post to publish at a later time, turn on **Schedule this post**, then select the date and time you want the post to publish.
   - If you want to publish your post on a recurring basis, from the “Repeats” dropdown menu, select how often you want your post to show up, such as weekly, monthly, or a custom schedule.
6. When you’re ready to publish, at the bottom right, select **Post**.
   - To post across multiple Business Profiles, select the profiles where you want to share it, then select **Post**.
   - To share the post on one profile, select **Skip**.

**Tip:** To improve your posts, you can add details like a descriptive title, start and end date, and other relevant info. If details change, you can always edit a post to keep info up-to-date.

## Edit or delete a post

You can edit or delete a post after you publish it.

1. [Go to your Business Profile](https://business.google.com/).
2. Select **Posts**.
3. At the right of the post you want to edit or delete:
   - To edit a post, select More   **Edit**.
   - To delete a post, select More   **Delete**.

## Understand your post status

After you publish your post, Google reviews it to make sure it [meets our posts content policy](/business/answer/7213077). Your post’s visibility to customers depends on its status:

- **Live:** The post is public and shows on Search and Maps.
- **Pending:** The post doesn't show on Search or Maps. It’s either in the upload stage, the processing stage, or your profile is unverified.
- **Not approved:** The post doesn't show on Search or Maps. The question mark next to the status indicates which content policy the post violates.

## Tips to improve your posts

- **Keep it professional:** Avoid misspelled words, extra characters, text that adds no value, and auto-generated or distracting content.
- **Keep your posts respectful:** Do not use inappropriate or offensive language, images, or videos.
- **Only post links to websites you trust:** The system doesn’t allow links that lead to malware, viruses, phishing, or pornographic material.
- **Regulated goods and services:** You can use posts if you offer products or services in a regulated industry, but do not include content about the products. These industries usually include adult services, alcohol, tobacco, pharmaceutical products, recreational drugs, health and medical devices, gambling services, fireworks, weapons, and financial services.
- **Keep your posts family-friendly:** Do not post sexually suggestive or explicit content.
- **Add complete and relevant details:** Include a descriptive title, event start and end times, and offer info.
